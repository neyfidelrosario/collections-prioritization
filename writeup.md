## Quick wins the score underrates

ACCT-036 ($179,305.17, 10 days overdue) and ACCT-019 ($158,285.71, 11 days overdue): nearly $340,000 combined, barely two weeks late, each contacted only twice. The score ranks them lower because they aren't very late yet. But one phone call each could stop them from becoming next month's 90+ day problems.

## Stuck accounts: try something different

Three accounts show 6+ contact attempts with no payment in 60+ days and no promise: ACCT-027 ($77,094.93), ACCT-042 ($21,913.55), ACCT-005 ($19,215.52). Repeated attempts aren't working. My move: stop emailing and call directly, involve a supervisor, or offer a structured payment plan. The goal shifts from collecting the full balance to getting any movement.

## What the score can't see

Relationships. A ten-year client going through a rough quarter might deserve patience the numbers don't show. The ranking tells me where to start Monday morning; it doesn't replace judgment about who I'm calling.
